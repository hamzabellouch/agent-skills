# Topology

## Domain
Properties preserved under continuous deformation.

## Core Concepts
- Topological spaces, open/closed sets
- Continuity, homeomorphism
- Compactness, connectedness
- Homotopy, fundamental group
- Homology, cohomology

## Key Methods
- Constructing homeomorphisms
- Van Kampen's theorem
- Mayer–Vietoris sequence
- Euler characteristic

## Canonical Examples
- ℝ and ℝ² are not homeomorphic.
- Fundamental group of circle = ℤ.
- Torus: π₁ = ℤ × ℤ.

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing homotopy with homeomorphism
- Assuming all spaces are Hausdorff
- Ignoring base point in π₁

## Related Skills
Real Analysis, Differential Geometry, Category Theory
