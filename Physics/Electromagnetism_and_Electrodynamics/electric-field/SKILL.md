# Electric Field

**ID:** `em.electrostatics.field`  
**Domain:** Electromagnetism → Electrostatics  
**Difficulty:** 2/5

## Prerequisites
- em.electrostatics.coulomb

## Core Concepts
- electric field
- test charge
- field lines
- superposition of fields
- continuous charge distributions

## Key Equations
$$
\vec E = \vec F/q_0
$$
$$
\vec E = k\int \frac{dq}{r^2}\hat r
$$

## Methods
- Compute E from point charges and distributions
- Use symmetry to simplify integrals
- Draw field-line sketches

## Typical Problem Types
- E on the axis of a ring or rod
- E of a dipole
- E of an infinite line

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing E with force
- Forgetting vector integration for distributions
