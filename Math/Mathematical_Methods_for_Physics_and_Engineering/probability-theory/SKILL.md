# Probability Theory

**ID:** `math.probability`  
**Domain:** Mathematics → Probability & Statistics  
**Difficulty:** 2/5

## Prerequisites
- (none)

## Core Concepts
- sample space
- conditional probability
- independence
- random variables
- expectation
- variance

## Key Equations
$$
P(A|B) = \frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
$$
$$
\mathbb{E}[X] = \sum_x xp(x),\ \mathrm{Var}(X) = \mathbb{E}[X^2]-\mathbb{E}[X]^2
$$

## Methods
- Compute probabilities from the sample space
- Use Bayes' theorem
- Find expectation and variance

## Typical Problem Types
- Combinatorial probabilities
- Bayesian updates
- Random walks

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing independent and mutually exclusive
- Mixing up conditional directions
