# Geophysics, Atmosphere & Climate

## Solid Earth
- Structure: crust, mantle, outer core (liquid), inner core; PREM model; Moho
- Seismology: P (compressional) and S (shear, not through liquid) waves; v_P = sqrt((K+4μ/3)/ρ), v_S = sqrt(μ/ρ); travel-time curves; magnitude M_w = (2/3)log10(M0) - 6.07 (M0 in N·m); energy ×32 per unit magnitude; epicenter by triangulation; shadow zones reveal core
- Gravity: g ≈ 9.78–9.83 m/s²; geoid; isostasy (Airy/Pratt); gravity anomalies
- Geomagnetism: dipole field ≈ 25–65 μT at surface; dynamo in liquid iron outer core; reversals; secular variation; magnetosphere
- Heat flow ~ 47 TW; mantle convection; plate tectonics rates cm/yr; radiometric dating (U-Pb, K-Ar)

## Atmosphere & ocean
- Hydrostatic p(z) = p0 e^{-z/H}, H = RT/Mg ≈ 8 km; lapse rate dry 9.8 K/km (g/c_p)
- Coriolis f = 2Ω sinφ; geostrophic balance fv = -(1/ρ)∂p/∂x; Rossby number; jet streams; Hadley, Ferrel, polar cells; thermal wind
- Rossby waves, gravity waves, ENSO, monsoons; cyclones and fronts; convective instability (CAPE)
- Ocean: Ekman transport, thermohaline circulation, tides (equilibrium theory, lunar ≈ 2.2× solar), tsunami speed sqrt(gh)

## Radiation & climate
- Solar constant S = 1361 W/m²; equilibrium T: (1-A)S/4 = εσT⁴ → T_e ≈ 255 K for A = 0.3; greenhouse effect raises to ≈ 288 K
- CO₂ forcing ≈ 5.35 ln(C/C0) W/m²; climate sensitivity ≈ 2.5–4 K per doubling (likely range); feedbacks: water vapor, ice-albedo, lapse rate, clouds
- Energy-balance models, GCMs, parameterization, paleoclimate (Milankovitch cycles ~ 23, 41, 100 kyr), carbon cycle, sea-level rise (thermal expansion + ice)
- Always cite current IPCC assessments or recent data for quantitative climate claims since values are updated.
