# Blackbody Radiation and Planck's Law

**ID:** `qm.found.blackbody_planck`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Foundations  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- thermo.heat_transfer

## Core Concepts
- blackbody spectrum
- Wien's displacement law
- Stefan-Boltzmann law
- ultraviolet catastrophe
- quanta

## Key Equations
$$
u(\nu,T) = \frac{8\pi h\nu^3}{c^3}\frac{1}{e^{h\nu/k_BT}-1}
$$
$$
\lambda_{max}T = 2.898\times10^{-3}\ \text{m K}
$$
$$
P = \sigma A T^4
$$

## Methods
- Integrate the Planck distribution for total power
- Use Wien's law to find peak wavelength
- Compare classical (Rayleigh-Jeans) with Planck

## Typical Problem Types
- Peak wavelength of the Sun
- Cosmic microwave background temperature
- Stellar luminosity

## Common Pitfalls
- Using Rayleigh-Jeans at high frequency
- Confusing frequency and wavelength peaks
