# Euclidean Geometry

## Domain
Classical geometry of the plane and space.

## Core Concepts
- Points, lines, planes, angles
- Triangles, circles, polygons
- Congruence, similarity
- Areas, volumes

## Key Methods
- Pythagorean theorem
- Law of sines and cosines
- Power of a point
- Ceva, Menelaus theorems
- Coordinate and vector methods

## Canonical Examples
- a² + b² = c² for right triangles.
- Area of circle = πr²; circumference = 2πr.
- Sum of angles in triangle = 180°.

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming diagrams are to scale
- Confusing SSS with SSA (ambiguous case)
- Misapplying similarity ratios

## Related Skills
Analytic Geometry, Trigonometry, Topology
