# Superconductivity

**ID:** `cond.superconductivity`  
**Domain:** Condensed Matter → Quantum Phases  
**Difficulty:** 5/5

## Prerequisites
- cond.band_theory

## Core Concepts
- BCS theory
- Cooper pairs
- Meissner effect
- London equations
- Josephson effect

## Key Equations
$$
\lambda_L = \sqrt{\frac{m}{\mu_0 n_s e^2}}
$$
$$
\Delta \approx 1.76 k_B T_c
$$
$$
I = I_c\sin\phi\ \text{(Josephson)}
$$

## Methods
- Explain pairing via electron-phonon interaction
- Apply Meissner and London equations
- Analyze Josephson junctions

## Typical Problem Types
- Critical temperature and field
- Flux quantization
- SQUID operation

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing type-I and type-II
- Treating superconductivity as perfect conduction only
