# Escape Velocity and Binding Energy

**ID:** `mech.gravitation.escape_velocity`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Gravitation  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.gravitation.field_potential

## Core Concepts
- escape velocity
- binding energy
- energy diagram

## Key Equations
$$
v_{esc} = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}}
$$
$$
E_{bind} = \frac{GMm}{2R}\ \text{(circular orbit)}
$$

## Methods
- Set total mechanical energy to zero
- Solve for the launch speed
- Compare binding and kinetic energies

## Typical Problem Types
- Escape speed from a planet
- Escape from a circular orbit
- Black hole horizon (Newtonian estimate)

## Common Pitfalls
- Using local g instead of GM/R^2
- Confusing escape and orbital velocity
