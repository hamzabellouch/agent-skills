## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
\mathcal{E} = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}
$$
$$
\Phi_B = \int \vec B\cdot d\vec A
$$
$$
\mathcal{E} = BLv\ \text{(motional)}
$$
