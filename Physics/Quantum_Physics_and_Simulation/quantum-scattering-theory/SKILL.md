# Quantum Scattering Theory

**ID:** `qm.scattering.theory`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Scattering  
**Difficulty:** 5/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.formalism.operators

## Core Concepts
- cross section
- partial waves
- phase shifts
- Born approximation
- Rutherford scattering

## Key Equations
$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega} = |f(\theta)|^2
$$
$$
f_{Born}(\theta) = -\frac{m}{2\pi\hbar^2}\int V(r')e^{i\vec q\cdot\vec r'}d^3r'
$$

## Methods
- Compute the scattering amplitude
- Use Born approximation for weak potentials
- Expand in partial waves for low energy

## Typical Problem Types
- Rutherford cross section
- Hard-sphere scattering
- Optical theorem

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing cross section with amplitude
- Using Born approximation when it fails
