# De Broglie Waves and Matter Waves

**ID:** `qm.found.de_broglie`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Foundations  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.found.photoelectric

## Core Concepts
- wave-particle duality
- de Broglie wavelength
- Davisson-Germer
- electron diffraction

## Key Equations
$$
\lambda = \frac{h}{p}
$$
$$
E = h\nu
$$

## Methods
- Compute \lambda from momentum
- Use \lambda = h/\sqrt{2mK} for non-relativistic particles
- Explain diffraction experiments

## Typical Problem Types
- Electron diffraction wavelength
- Neutron diffraction
- Bohr quantization from standing waves

## Common Pitfalls
- Using classical wavelength
- Forgetting relativistic corrections for fast particles
