# Energy Physics

- Units: 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ; 1 toe = 41.9 GJ; world primary energy ≈ 600 EJ/yr (≈ 19 TW)
- Solar: irradiance 1000 W/m² peak at surface; PV efficiency 20–25% (Si), SQ limit 33%; capacity factor ~15–25%
- Wind: P = ½ρAv³C_p; Betz limit C_p ≤ 16/27 ≈ 0.593; capacity factor 25–50%
- Hydro: P = ηρgQh; tidal, wave, geothermal
- Heat engines: Carnot limit; coal/gas plant 35–60% (combined cycle ~60%); CHP; heat pumps COP = Q_h/W (≈ 3–5)
- Nuclear fission: 8e13 J/kg U-235 (complete fission); reactors types PWR, BWR, CANDU, SMR; waste half-lives; fusion prospects
- Batteries: Li-ion ≈ 150–300 Wh/kg, cycle life; energy density vs power density; Nernst equation for cells; solid-state, sodium-ion, flow batteries; round-trip efficiency; degradation
- Hydrogen & fuel cells: electrolysis ~ 50–70% efficient; fuel cell ~ 40–60%; LHV hydrogen 120 MJ/kg, but low volumetric density
- Storage: pumped hydro (70–85% RT), compressed air, flywheels, thermal storage
- Grid physics: AC power P = VI cosφ, 3-phase, frequency stability (inertia), transmission losses I²R (why high voltage), HVDC
- Metrics: EROI, LCOE, capacity factor, power density (W/m²)
- Always state assumptions and use current published costs/efficiencies; they change quickly.
