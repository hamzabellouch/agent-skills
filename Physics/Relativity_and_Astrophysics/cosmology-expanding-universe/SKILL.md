# Cosmology and the Expanding Universe

**ID:** `ast.cosmology`  
**Domain:** Astrophysics & Cosmology → Cosmology  
**Difficulty:** 5/5

## Prerequisites
- rel.general.geodesics
- rel.special.doppler

## Core Concepts
- Friedmann equations
- scale factor
- Hubble law
- CMB
- dark energy

## Key Equations
$$
\left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho - \frac{kc^2}{a^2}
$$
$$
v = H_0 d
$$
$$
1+z = \frac{a_0}{a}
$$

## Methods
- Apply the Friedmann equations
- Use redshift to infer distances
- Analyze the CMB and structure growth

## Typical Problem Types
- Age of the universe
- Critical density
- CMB temperature

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing comoving and physical distances
- Using Newtonian intuition for cosmological redshift
