# Astrophysics

## Stars
- Hydrostatic equilibrium dP/dr = -Gmρ/r²; mass conservation; energy transport (radiative, convective); virial theorem 2K + U = 0
- Luminosity L = 4πR²σT⁴; main sequence L ∝ M^{3.5}, lifetime t ≈ 10 Gyr (M/M☉)^{-2.5}
- Magnitudes m - M = 5 log10(d/10 pc); color index B-V; distance via parallax d[pc] = 1/p[arcsec]; standard candles (Cepheids, SN Ia)
- Nuclear burning: pp chain (T ~ 1.5e7 K), CNO, triple-alpha (1e8 K), C, O, Si burning; iron core collapse
- Evolution: protostar → main sequence → red giant → helium flash/AGB → white dwarf (M < 8 M☉); massive stars → core-collapse supernova
- Chandrasekhar limit ≈ 1.4 M☉; neutron stars ≈ 1.4–2.2 M☉, R ≈ 12 km; TOV limit; pulsars (magnetic dipole spin-down); magnetars; black holes from collapse
- Mass-radius for white dwarfs R ∝ M^{-1/3}

## Binaries & accretion
- Roche lobe, mass transfer, X-ray binaries, Eddington luminosity L_Edd = 4πGMm_pc/σ_T ≈ 1.26e31 W (M/M☉)
- Accretion disks (Shakura-Sunyaev), jets, AGN, quasars; gravitational wave mergers

## Galaxies & medium
- Rotation curves flat → dark matter halos; Tully-Fisher; Hubble sequence; supermassive black hole Sgr A* ≈ 4e6 M☉; M-σ relation
- Interstellar medium: HII regions, molecular clouds, Jeans mass M_J ∝ T^{3/2}ρ^{-1/2}; star formation; Strömgren sphere

## Radiative processes & observation
- Blackbody, bremsstrahlung, synchrotron, inverse Compton; opacity and optical depth τ, I = I0 e^{-τ}; spectral lines, redshift z = Δλ/λ
- Telescopes: resolution θ = 1.22λ/D, adaptive optics, interferometry (VLBI, EHT), space observatories; multi-messenger (neutrinos, GW)
- Exoplanets: transit depth (R_p/R_*)², radial velocity K, direct imaging, habitable zone, atmospheres via transmission spectra
