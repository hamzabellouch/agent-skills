## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
a\sin\theta = m\lambda\ \text{(single-slit minima)}
$$
$$
d\sin\theta = m\lambda\ \text{(grating maxima)}
$$
$$
\theta_{min} = 1.22\frac{\lambda}{D}
$$
