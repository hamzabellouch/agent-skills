# Game Theory

## Domain
Strategic interaction between rational agents.

## Core Concepts
- Normal-form and extensive-form games
- Nash equilibrium
- Dominant strategies
- Mixed strategies
- Cooperative games, Shapley value

## Key Methods
- Backward induction
- Minimax theorem
- Best-response dynamics
- Mechanism design

## Canonical Examples
- Prisoner's dilemma: (Defect, Defect) is unique NE.
- Matching pennies: mixed NE with p=1/2.
- Shapley value for fair coalition payoff.

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming unique equilibrium
- Ignoring information asymmetry
- Confusing Pareto optimality with NE

## Related Skills
Optimization, Probability, Economics
