# Probability Theory

## Domain
Randomness and uncertainty.

## Core Concepts
- Sample spaces, events, σ-algebras
- Random variables (discrete, continuous)
- Distributions: Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, Normal, Exponential
- Expectation, variance, moments
- Independence, conditional probability, Bayes

## Key Methods
- Law of total probability
- Bayes' theorem
- Central Limit Theorem
- Law of large numbers
- Moment generating functions

## Canonical Examples
- P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A)/P(B).
- Sum of n iid Bernoulli(p) ~ Binomial(n,p).
- CLT: (X̄-μ)/(σ/√n) → N(0,1).

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing P(A|B) with P(B|A)
- Assuming independence without justification
- Misusing expectation of products
