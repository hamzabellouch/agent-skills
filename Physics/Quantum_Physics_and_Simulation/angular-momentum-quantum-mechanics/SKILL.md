# Angular Momentum in Quantum Mechanics

**ID:** `qm.formalism.angular_momentum`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Formalism  
**Difficulty:** 5/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.formalism.operators

## Core Concepts
- L², L_z eigenstates
- spherical harmonics
- addition of angular momenta
- Clebsch-Gordan

## Key Equations
$$
[L_i, L_j] = i\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}L_k
$$
$$
L^2|l,m\rangle = \hbar^2 l(l+1)|l,m\rangle
$$
$$
L_z|l,m\rangle = \hbar m|l,m\rangle
$$

## Methods
- Use ladder operators L_±
- Combine angular momenta via Clebsch-Gordan
- Apply to central potentials

## Typical Problem Types
- Eigenvalues of L² and L_z
- Spin-orbit coupling
- Molecular rotation spectra

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting the l(l+1) factor
- Wrong allowed range of m
