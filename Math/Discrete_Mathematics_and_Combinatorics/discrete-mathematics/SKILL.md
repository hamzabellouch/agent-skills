# Discrete Mathematics

## Domain
Finite and countable structures.

## Core Concepts
- Sets, relations, functions
- Induction, recursion
- Boolean algebra, logic gates
- Recurrence relations
- Automata and formal languages

## Key Methods
- Mathematical induction (weak, strong)
- Generating functions
- Master theorem for recurrences
- Pigeonhole principle

## Canonical Examples
- Fibonacci: F(n) = F(n-1) + F(n-2), closed form via Binet.
- Prove Σ_{k=1}^n k = n(n+1)/2 by induction.
- 2^n subsets of an n-element set.

## Common Pitfalls
- Missing base cases in induction
- Confusing strong and weak induction
- Off-by-one in indexing

## Related Skills
Combinatorics, Graph Theory, Logic
