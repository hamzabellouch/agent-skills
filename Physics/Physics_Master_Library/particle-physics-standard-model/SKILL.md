# Particle Physics & the Standard Model

## Content
- Fermions: 3 generations of quarks (u,d; c,s; t,b) and leptons (e,νe; μ,νμ; τ,ντ); antiparticles
- Gauge bosons: γ (EM), W±, Z (weak), 8 gluons (strong); Higgs boson (125 GeV, discovered 2012)
- Gauge group SU(3)_C × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y; Higgs mechanism gives W, Z, fermion masses; sinθ_W² ≈ 0.231
- Conservation laws: energy-momentum, charge, baryon number, lepton numbers (violated by neutrino oscillations), CPT; parity violated by weak interaction; CP violation (CKM phase, K and B mesons)
- QCD: color, confinement, asymptotic freedom, running α_s, hadrons (mesons q q̄, baryons qqq), exotic states (tetraquarks, pentaquarks), jets, parton distribution functions, lattice QCD
- Electroweak: V-A structure, CKM matrix, Z-pole precision tests, muon lifetime τ ∝ 1/G_F² m_μ⁵
- Neutrinos: mixing PMNS matrix, oscillation P = sin²2θ sin²(1.27 Δm²[eV²] L[km]/E[GeV]), mass hierarchy open, absolute mass < ~0.8 eV, Dirac vs Majorana, neutrinoless double-beta decay

## Calculation recipe
1. Check conservation laws to decide if a process is allowed and which force mediates it.
2. Draw Feynman diagram; count vertices (each ~√α).
3. Rate/cross section: σ ∝ |M|² × phase space; decay width Γ = ħ/τ; Breit-Wigner for resonances.
4. Kinematics: use invariant mass; CM energy √s; fixed-target √s ≈ sqrt(2 m E_beam).
5. Natural units ħ = c = 1; conversion ħc = 197.327 MeV·fm.

## Experiments
- Accelerators: LHC (13.6 TeV pp), linear vs circular, synchrotron losses ∝ E⁴/(m⁴R)
- Detectors: tracker, calorimeters (EM, hadronic), muon system, trigger
- Statistics: 5σ discovery, p-values, look-elsewhere effect

## Beyond SM
Dark matter candidates (WIMPs, axions, sterile neutrinos), supersymmetry, grand unification, strong CP problem, hierarchy problem, baryogenesis (Sakharov conditions), g-2 tensions.
