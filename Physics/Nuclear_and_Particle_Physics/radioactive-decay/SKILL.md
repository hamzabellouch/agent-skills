# Radioactive Decay

**ID:** `nucl.radioactivity`  
**Domain:** Nuclear & Particle → Nuclear  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.schrodinger.tunneling

## Core Concepts
- decay constant
- half-life
- alpha/beta/gamma decay
- activity
- exponential decay law

## Key Equations
$$
N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}
$$
$$
t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\lambda}
$$
$$
A = \lambda N
$$

## Methods
- Apply the decay law
- Use half-life to find \lambda
- Track parent-daughter chains

## Typical Problem Types
- Carbon dating
- Medical isotope activity
- Decay chains

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing half-life and mean lifetime
- Forgetting the branching ratio
