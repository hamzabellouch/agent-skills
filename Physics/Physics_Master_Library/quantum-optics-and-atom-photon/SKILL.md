# Quantum Optics

- Field quantization: E = Σ sqrt(ħω/2ε0V)(a e^{-iωt} + a† e^{iωt}); H = ħω(a†a + ½)
- States: Fock |n⟩; coherent |α⟩ (Poissonian, ⟨n⟩ = |α|²); thermal; squeezed (ΔX1ΔX2 ≥ 1/4 with one reduced); Schrödinger cat states
- Photon statistics: g²(0) = 1 coherent, 2 thermal, 0 for ideal single photon; Hanbury Brown–Twiss experiment
- Jaynes-Cummings: H = ħω a†a + (ħω_a/2)σ_z + ħg(aσ+ + a†σ-); vacuum Rabi splitting 2g; collapse and revival; strong coupling when g > κ, γ
- Cavity QED, circuit QED (superconducting qubits + resonators), Purcell effect
- Entangled photons: SPDC; polarization-entangled pairs for Bell tests; Hong-Ou-Mandel dip; boson sampling
- Quantum measurement: homodyne detection, quantum non-demolition, standard quantum limit, squeezed light in LIGO
- Optomechanics: radiation pressure, sideband cooling to the ground state, quantum limits of force sensing
- Quantum communication: single-photon sources, quantum memories, repeaters, satellite QKD
