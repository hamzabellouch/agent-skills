# Rotational Kinetic Energy and Work

**ID:** `mech.rotation.rot_energy`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Rotation  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.rotation.moment_of_inertia
- mech.energy.work

## Core Concepts
- rotational KE
- rotational work
- power in rotation
- combined translation and rotation

## Key Equations
$$
K_{rot} = \tfrac12 I\omega^2
$$
$$
W = \int \tau\, d\theta
$$
$$
K = \tfrac12 mv^2 + \tfrac12 I\omega^2
$$

## Methods
- Split energy into translational and rotational parts
- Use energy conservation if no dissipation
- Include both terms for rolling bodies

## Typical Problem Types
- Rolling cylinder down a ramp
- Pulley with a falling mass
- Flywheel energy storage

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting the rotational term
- Using v instead of \omega for rotational KE
