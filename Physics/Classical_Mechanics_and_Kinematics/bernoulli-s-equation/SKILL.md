# Bernoulli's Equation

**ID:** `mech.fluids.bernoulli`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Fluids  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.fluids.continuity
- mech.energy.conservation

## Core Concepts
- Bernoulli's principle
- streamline flow
- dynamic pressure
- Venturi effect
- lift

## Key Equations
$$
P + \tfrac12\rho v^2 + \rho g h = \text{const}
$$

## Methods
- Apply along a streamline for steady, inviscid, incompressible flow
- Combine with continuity
- Identify which terms drop out

## Typical Problem Types
- Venturi meter
- Torricelli's theorem
- Airplane wing (qualitative)

## Common Pitfalls
- Applying Bernoulli across a viscous or turbulent region
- Forgetting the height term
