# Soft Matter & Materials Physics

- Elasticity: Hooke σ = Eε; shear modulus G = E/2(1+ν); bulk modulus K = E/3(1-2ν); Poisson ratio ν; beam bending deflection δ = FL³/3EI; buckling F_cr = π²EI/(KL)²
- Stress tensor, principal stresses, von Mises yield; plasticity and dislocations; work hardening; fatigue (S-N curves, Paris law); Griffith fracture σ_f = sqrt(2Eγ/πa)
- Viscoelasticity: Maxwell and Kelvin-Voigt models; storage G′ and loss G″ moduli; Deborah number
- Rheology: Newtonian vs shear-thinning/thickening, yield-stress fluids (Bingham), thixotropy
- Polymers: Flory theory R ~ N^{3/5}, glass transition T_g, rubber elasticity (entropic), reptation (Doi-Edwards)
- Colloids: Brownian motion, DLVO theory, depletion forces, self-assembly, crystallization, active matter (self-propelled particles, flocking Vicsek model, MIPS)
- Liquid crystals: nematic order parameter S = ⟨(3cos²θ - 1)/2⟩, Frank elastic constants, Fréedericksz transition, LCDs
- Granular: jamming, angle of repose, Janssen effect, Brazil-nut effect
- Glasses: non-ergodic, Kauzmann paradox, mode-coupling theory, aging
- Nanomaterials: graphene (E ≈ 1 TPa), carbon nanotubes, quantum dots, 2D materials, metamaterials (negative index, cloaking), photonic crystals, phononic crystals
- Characterization: XRD, SEM/TEM, AFM, scattering (SAXS, SANS), DSC, tensile testing
