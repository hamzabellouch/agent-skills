# Gravitational Field and Potential

**ID:** `mech.gravitation.field_potential`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Gravitation  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.gravitation.newton_law
- mech.energy.conservative_potential

## Core Concepts
- gravitational field
- gravitational potential
- potential energy
- field lines
- shell theorem

## Key Equations
$$
\vec g = -G\frac{M}{r^2}\hat r
$$
$$
U = -G\frac{Mm}{r}
$$
$$
\Phi = -G\frac{M}{r}
$$

## Methods
- Compute ec g as force per unit mass
- Find U from F = -dU/dr
- Use shell theorem for spherical mass distributions

## Typical Problem Types
- Field of a planet
- Escape energy
- Potential inside a uniform sphere

## Common Pitfalls
- Using U = mgh at large r
- Forgetting the negative sign in U
