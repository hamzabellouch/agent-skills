# Spin and Magnetic Moments

**ID:** `qm.formalism.spin`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Formalism  
**Difficulty:** 5/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.formalism.angular_momentum

## Core Concepts
- spin-1/2
- Pauli matrices
- Stern-Gerlach
- spin-orbit coupling
- magnetic moment

## Key Equations
$$
\vec S = \frac{\hbar}{2}\vec\sigma
$$
$$
\mu_s = -g_s\frac{e}{2m}\vec S
$$

## Methods
- Use Pauli matrices for spin-1/2
- Compute expectation values of spin
- Include spin in the Hamiltonian (Zeeman, spin-orbit)

## Typical Problem Types
- Stern-Gerlach splitting
- Zeeman effect
- Fine structure of hydrogen

## Common Pitfalls
- Treating spin as classical rotation
- Forgetting g_s ≈ 2
