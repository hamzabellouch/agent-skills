# Buoyancy and Archimedes' Principle

**ID:** `mech.fluids.buoyancy`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Fluids  
**Difficulty:** 2/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.fluids.statics

## Core Concepts
- buoyant force
- displaced fluid
- floating
- apparent weight
- density

## Key Equations
$$
F_B = \rho_{fluid} V_{disp}\, g
$$

## Methods
- Identify the displaced volume
- Balance buoyant and gravitational forces for floating
- Use apparent weight for submerged objects

## Typical Problem Types
- Floating ship
- Iceberg
- Object submerged by a string

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing displaced volume with total volume
- Forgetting that floating objects displace their own weight
