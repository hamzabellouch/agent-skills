# Acoustics

- Speed of sound in air c ≈ 331 + 0.6 T(°C) m/s; liquids/solids c = sqrt(B/ρ)
- Pressure amplitude p, intensity I = p²/(2ρc); SPL = 20 log10(p_rms/20 μPa); doubling distance from point source → -6 dB; two equal sources +3 dB
- Impedance Z = ρc; reflection R = ((Z2-Z1)/(Z2+Z1))²; why ultrasound needs gel
- Standing waves: closed pipe f_n = (2n-1)c/4L; open pipe f_n = nc/2L; end correction ≈ 0.6 r
- Strings f_n = (n/2L) sqrt(T/μ); membranes (drums) Bessel-function modes; plates Chladni patterns
- Helmholtz resonator f = (c/2π) sqrt(A/(V L_eff))
- Room acoustics: Sabine RT60 = 0.161 V/A (A = Σ α_i S_i); modes f = (c/2) sqrt((nx/Lx)²+(ny/Ly)²+(nz/Lz)²); Schroeder frequency
- Psychoacoustics: audible 20 Hz–20 kHz; equal-loudness (Fletcher-Munson); A-weighting; masking; pitch vs frequency; timbre from harmonics
- Doppler, shock waves (Mach cone sinθ = c/v), sonic boom
- Ultrasound: medical imaging (resolution ~ λ), attenuation ∝ f, Doppler flow; sonar equation; acoustic levitation; sonoluminescence
- Noise control: absorption, barriers, mass law (+6 dB per mass doubling), active noise cancellation (anti-phase)
- Nonlinear acoustics and thermoacoustics (engines/refrigerators); acoustic metamaterials
