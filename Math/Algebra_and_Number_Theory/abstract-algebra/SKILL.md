# Abstract Algebra

## Domain
Algebraic structures: groups, rings, fields.

## Core Concepts
- Groups: axioms, subgroups, cosets, quotients
- Homomorphisms, isomorphisms, kernels
- Rings, ideals, quotient rings
- Fields, field extensions, Galois theory
- Modules and algebras

## Key Methods
- Lagrange's theorem: |H| divides |G|
- Sylow theorems for finite groups
- First isomorphism theorem: G/ker(φ) ≅ im(φ)
- Constructing splitting fields

## Canonical Examples
- Z/nZ is a field iff n is prime.
- S₃ has 6 elements; subgroups have orders 1,2,3,6.
- Galois group of x²-2 over Q is Z/2Z.

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming all groups are abelian
- Confusing ring homomorphism with group homomorphism
- Forgetting units vs. non-units in rings
