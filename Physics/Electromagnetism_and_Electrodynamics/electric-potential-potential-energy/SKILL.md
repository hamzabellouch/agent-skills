# Electric Potential and Potential Energy

**ID:** `em.electrostatics.potential`  
**Domain:** Electromagnetism → Electrostatics  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- em.electrostatics.field
- mech.energy.conservative_potential

## Core Concepts
- electric potential
- potential difference
- equipotentials
- potential of point charge/dipole
- gradient relation

## Key Equations
$$
V = k\frac{q}{r}
$$
$$
\vec E = -\nabla V
$$
$$
U = qV
$$

## Methods
- Compute V from charges
- Use E = -
abla V to get fields
- Apply energy conservation for charges

## Typical Problem Types
- Potential on the axis of a ring
- Potential of a dipole
- Energy to assemble charge configurations

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing potential and potential energy
- Forgetting the minus sign in E = -
abla V
