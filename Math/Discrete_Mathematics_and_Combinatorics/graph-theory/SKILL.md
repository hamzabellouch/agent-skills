# Graph Theory

## Domain
Graphs, networks, and their properties.

## Core Concepts
- Vertices, edges, degree, paths, cycles
- Trees, forests, bipartite graphs
- Connectivity, planarity
- Coloring, matching
- Spectral graph theory

## Key Methods
- BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, Bellman–Ford
- Kruskal, Prim (MST)
- Max-flow min-cut
- Euler and Hamiltonian path criteria

## Canonical Examples
- K₅ is non-planar (Kuratowski).
- Every tree with n vertices has n-1 edges.
- Four-color theorem for planar graphs.

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing Eulerian vs. Hamiltonian
- Assuming all graphs are simple
- Ignoring directed vs. undirected

## Related Skills
Combinatorics, Optimization, Discrete Math
