# Four-Vectors and Covariant Formalism

**ID:** `rel.special.four_vectors`  
**Domain:** Relativity → Special  
**Difficulty:** 5/5

## Prerequisites
- rel.special.lorentz
- math.tensor_calculus

## Core Concepts
- four-vector
- four-momentum
- four-current
- Lorentz invariant
- index notation

## Key Equations
$$
p^\mu = (E/c, \vec p)
$$
$$
x^\mu = (ct, \vec x)
$$
$$
p^\mu p_\mu = -m^2 c^2
$$

## Methods
- Write physical quantities as four-vectors
- Contract indices for invariants
- Use the metric signature consistently

## Typical Problem Types
- Invariant mass from four-momenta
- Covariant form of Maxwell's equations
- Relativistic kinematics

## Common Pitfalls
- Sign convention errors in the metric
- Forgetting to sum over repeated indices
