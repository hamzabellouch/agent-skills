# 1D Motion with Constant Acceleration

**ID:** `mech.kinematics.constant_acceleration`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Kinematics  
**Difficulty:** 1/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.kinematics.definitions

## Core Concepts
- uniform acceleration
- suvat equations
- free fall
- sign conventions

## Key Equations
$$
v = v_0 + at
$$
$$
x = x_0 + v_0 t + \tfrac12 a t^2
$$
$$
v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x-x_0)
$$
$$
x = x_0 + \tfrac12(v_0+v)t
$$

## Methods
- Identify knowns/unknowns and choose the suvat equation missing the unwanted variable
- Split motion into phases when acceleration changes
- Use g = 9.81 m/s^2 downward for free fall

## Typical Problem Types
- Free-fall problems
- Braking distance
- Multi-phase motion

## Common Pitfalls
- Using suvat when acceleration is not constant
- Forgetting initial position x_0
