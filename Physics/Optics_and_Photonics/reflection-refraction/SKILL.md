## Key Equations

## Methods
- Apply Snell's law at each interface
- Use Fermat's principle for path problems
- Track the light ray geometry

## Typical Problem Types
- Refraction through a slab
- Mirages

## Common Pitfalls

- Mixing indices across interfaces
