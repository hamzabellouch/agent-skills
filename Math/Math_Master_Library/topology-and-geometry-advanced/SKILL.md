# Topology & Advanced Geometry

## Point-set topology
- Topological space (open sets), basis, subspace/product/quotient topologies; closure, interior, boundary.
- Continuity = preimages of opens are open; homeomorphism; Hausdorff, regular, normal; metrizable.
- Compactness (every open cover has a finite subcover; Tychonoff product theorem), connectedness, path-connectedness; Urysohn lemma, Tietze extension.
- Examples: Zariski, order, cofinite, Sorgenfrey line, topologist's sine curve.

## Algebraic topology
- Homotopy equivalence; fundamental group pi_1: pi_1(S^1) = Z, pi_1(torus) = Z^2; van Kampen theorem; covering spaces and the lifting criterion; classification of surfaces.
- Homology H_n (simplicial, singular, cellular): Betti numbers; Euler characteristic chi = sum (-1)^n b_n (sphere 2, torus 0); Mayer-Vietoris; cohomology and cup product; Poincare duality; Brouwer fixed-point theorem; Borsuk-Ulam; hairy ball theorem.
- Higher homotopy groups, fibrations, K-theory, spectral sequences (overview).
- Knot theory: Reidemeister moves, Jones polynomial, knot invariants. Topological data analysis: persistent homology.

## Manifolds & differential geometry
- Smooth manifolds, charts, tangent spaces, vector fields, Lie bracket, differential forms, exterior derivative d, Stokes theorem, de Rham cohomology.
- Riemannian metric g, geodesics, Levi-Civita connection, curvature tensor, sectional/Ricci/scalar curvature; Gauss-Bonnet: integral K dA + boundary = 2 pi chi; Gauss's Theorema Egregium; Hopf-Rinow.
- Lie groups and Lie algebras; fiber bundles and connections (gauge theory link).
- Surfaces in R^3: first and second fundamental form, principal curvatures, minimal surfaces; Poincare conjecture (Perelman via Ricci flow).

## Algebraic geometry (overview)
Affine varieties V(I), Nullstellensatz, Zariski topology, projective space, Bezout's theorem (intersection number = product of degrees), schemes, sheaves, elliptic curves, Riemann-Roch.

## Intuition
Topology ignores distances: a coffee cup equals a donut; use invariants (pi_1, H_n, chi, orientability) to prove spaces are not equivalent.
