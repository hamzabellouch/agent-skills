# Differential Geometry

## Domain
Geometry of smooth curves and surfaces.

## Core Concepts
- Curves: curvature, torsion, Frenet–Serret
- Surfaces: first and second fundamental forms
- Gaussian and mean curvature
- Manifolds, tangent bundles
- Riemannian metrics, geodesics

## Key Methods
- Parametrization and arc length
- Christoffel symbols
- Gauss–Bonnet theorem
- Covariant derivatives

## Canonical Examples
- Sphere has constant Gaussian curvature 1/R².
- Geodesics on sphere are great circles.
- Gauss–Bonnet: ∫K dA = 2πχ(M).

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing intrinsic vs. extrinsic curvature
- Coordinate-dependent statements
- Ignoring orientation

## Related Skills
Topology, Multivariable Calculus, General Relativity
