# Second Law of Thermodynamics

**ID:** `thermo.second_law`  
**Domain:** Thermodynamics → Laws  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- thermo.first_law

## Core Concepts
- entropy
- Kelvin-Planck
- Clausius
- irreversibility
- reversible process

## Key Equations
$$
\Delta S \ge \int \frac{dQ}{T}
$$
$$
\Delta S_{universe} \ge 0
$$

## Methods
- Determine whether a process is reversible
- Compute \Delta S along a reversible path
- Apply Carnot efficiency as the upper bound

## Typical Problem Types
- Mixing of hot and cold water
- Free expansion of a gas
- Heat engine efficiency limit

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming entropy always increases for a subsystem (need universe)
- Using irreversible paths to compute \Delta S directly
