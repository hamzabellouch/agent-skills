# Finite Square Well and Bound States

**ID:** `qm.schrodinger.finite_well`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Schrödinger  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.schrodinger.particle_in_box

## Core Concepts
- finite well
- evanescent tails
- bound vs scattering states
- transcendental eigenvalue equation

## Key Equations
$$
\tan(ka) = \frac{\kappa}{k}\ \text{(even)}
$$
$$
k^2 + \kappa^2 = \frac{2mV_0}{\hbar^2}
$$

## Methods
- Solve matching conditions at the walls
- Solve the transcendental equation graphically/numerically
- Identify the number of bound states from V_0 a^2

## Typical Problem Types
- Number of bound states in a well
- Penetration depth of the tail
- Deuteron as a shallow bound state

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming infinite-well wavefunctions
- Forgetting the exponentially decaying tail
