# Conservation of Mechanical Energy

**ID:** `mech.energy.conservation`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Energy  
**Difficulty:** 2/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.energy.conservative_potential

## Core Concepts
- conservation of energy
- mechanical energy
- energy diagrams
- turning points

## Key Equations
$$
E = K + U = \text{const}
$$
$$
\tfrac12 m v^2 + U(x) = E
$$

## Methods
- Check that only conservative forces do work
- Write E before = E after
- Solve for the target

## Typical Problem Types
- Roller coaster
- Pendulum speed at the bottom
- Block-spring system

## Common Pitfalls
- Applying conservation when friction is present
- Wrong reference height for PE
