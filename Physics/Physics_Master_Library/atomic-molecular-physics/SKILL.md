# Atomic & Molecular Physics

## Atoms
- Hydrogen-like E_n = -13.6 Z²/n² eV; Rydberg 1/λ = R(1/n1² - 1/n2²), R = 1.097e7 m⁻¹
- Quantum defect for alkalis; shell filling (Madelung rule)
- LS coupling: term symbols ^{2S+1}L_J; Hund's rules (max S, then max L, then J = |L-S| for less-than-half-filled shells, L+S for more)
- Lande g_J = 1 + [J(J+1)+S(S+1)-L(L+1)]/[2J(J+1)]
- E1 selection rules: Δl = ±1, ΔJ = 0,±1 (not 0↔0), parity changes
- Hartree-Fock, helium (orthohelium vs parahelium), Thomas-Fermi

## Light-matter and cold atoms
- Einstein A and B; natural linewidth Γ = 1/τ; Doppler broadening Δν/ν = sqrt(8kT ln2/mc²)
- Doppler cooling limit T_D = ħΓ/2k_B; recoil limit; magneto-optical trap; optical molasses; optical tweezers and lattices
- Bose-Einstein condensates; Feshbach resonances; atomic clocks (Cs 9 192 631 770 Hz) and optical clocks
- Rydberg atoms, quantum simulation with atoms

## Molecules
- Born-Oppenheimer: separate electronic and nuclear motion
- H2+ and H2: LCAO, bonding/antibonding, molecular orbital diagrams; bond order
- Rotational levels E_J = BJ(J+1), B = ħ²/2I, lines spaced 2B; vibrational E_v = ħω(v+½) with anharmonic correction; Morse potential
- Selection rules: rotational ΔJ = ±1 (permanent dipole), vibrational Δv = ±1 (changing dipole); Raman ΔJ = 0,±2
- Franck-Condon principle; fluorescence vs phosphorescence; electronic term symbols Σ, Π, Δ
- Spectroscopy techniques: absorption, emission, Raman, NMR (ν = γB/2π), EPR, mass spectrometry
