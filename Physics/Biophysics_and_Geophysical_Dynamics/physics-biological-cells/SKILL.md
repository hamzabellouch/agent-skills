# Physics of Biological Cells

**ID:** `bio.physics_cells`  
**Domain:** Interdisciplinary → Biophysics  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.fluid.viscosity
- stat.mech.boltzmann

## Core Concepts
- Brownian motion
- diffusion
- osmotic pressure
- membrane mechanics
- molecular motors

## Key Equations
$$
\langle x^2\rangle = 2Dt
$$
$$
D = \frac{k_BT}{6\pi\eta a}
$$
$$
\Pi = cRT
$$

## Methods
- Use the diffusion equation at cellular scales
- Compute Stokes drag on proteins
- Balance osmotic and mechanical pressures

## Typical Problem Types
- Diffusion time across a cell
- Sedimentation of ribosomes
- Vesicle mechanics

## Common Pitfalls
- Using macroscopic fluid intuition at molecular scales
- Ignoring thermal fluctuations

## Related Skills
- stat.mech.fluctuations
- mech.fluid.viscosity
