# Electromagnetic Waves

**ID:** `em.waves.em_waves`  
**Domain:** Electromagnetism → Waves  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- em.maxwell.equations
- mech.waves.wave_equation

## Core Concepts
- plane wave
- polarization
- transverse nature
- speed of light
- index of refraction

## Key Equations
$$
\nabla^2\vec E = \mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{\partial^2 \vec E}{\partial t^2}
$$
$$
c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}
$$
$$
v = \frac{c}{n}
$$

## Methods
- Derive the wave equation from Maxwell
- Identify polarization and propagation direction
- Apply boundary conditions at interfaces

## Typical Problem Types
- Plane-wave propagation
- Reflection/refraction at boundaries
- Radiation pressure

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming E and B are independent
- Forgetting the transverse constraint
