## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
f_s \le \mu_s N
$$
$$
f_k = \mu_k N
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Analytical & Problem-Solving Methods
1. **Compute normal force first**
2. **Check if static friction is sufficient to prevent motion**
3. **Switch to kinetic friction once sliding starts**

## Standard Problem Archetypes & Applications
- **Block on a horizontal surface pulled by a force**
- **Block on an incline at the angle of repose**
- **Two stacked blocks**

---

## Common Pitfalls, Edge Cases & Anti-Patterns
- > [!WARNING]
  > Treating static friction as always equal to \mu_s N
- > [!WARNING]
  > Forgetting that static friction adjusts to the applied force
