## Key Equations
$$
B = [Zm_p + Nm_n - M]c^2
$$
$$
B(A,Z) = a_V A - a_S A^{2/3} - a_C\frac{Z^2}{A^{1/3}} - a_A\frac{(A-2Z)^2}{A} \pm \delta
$$
