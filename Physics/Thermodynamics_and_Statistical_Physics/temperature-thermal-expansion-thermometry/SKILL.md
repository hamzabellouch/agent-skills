# Temperature, Thermal Expansion and Thermometry

**ID:** `thermo.temperature_expansion`  
**Domain:** Thermodynamics → Foundations  
**Difficulty:** 1/5

## Prerequisites
- (none)

## Core Concepts
- temperature scales
- thermal expansion
- thermometers
- absolute zero
- linear/volume expansion

## Key Equations
$$
L = L_0(1+\alpha\Delta T)
$$
$$
V = V_0(1+\beta\Delta T)
$$
$$
\beta \approx 3\alpha
$$

## Methods
- Convert between Celsius, Kelvin, Fahrenheit
- Use the appropriate expansion coefficient
- Account for bimetallic strips

## Typical Problem Types
- Expansion of a rod
- Bimetallic strip bending
- Volume expansion of a liquid

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting \Delta T is in Kelvin (same as Celsius for differences)
- Mixing linear and volumetric coefficients
