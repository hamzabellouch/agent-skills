# Bohr Model of the Atom

**ID:** `qm.found.bohr`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Foundations  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.found.photoelectric
- em.electrostatics.coulomb

## Core Concepts
- stationary orbits
- quantized angular momentum
- energy levels
- Rydberg formula
- spectral lines

## Key Equations
$$
L = n\hbar
$$
$$
E_n = -\frac{13.6\ \text{eV}}{n^2}
$$
$$
\frac{1}{\lambda} = R\left(\frac{1}{n_f^2}-\frac{1}{n_i^2}\right)
$$

## Methods
- Quantize angular momentum
- Balance Coulomb and centripetal forces
- Compute transition energies

## Typical Problem Types
- Hydrogen spectrum
- Ionization energy
- Positronium analogue

## Common Pitfalls
- Treating Bohr model as fully quantum
- Forgetting it only works for hydrogen-like atoms
