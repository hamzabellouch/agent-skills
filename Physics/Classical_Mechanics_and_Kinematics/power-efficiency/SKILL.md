# Power and Efficiency

**ID:** `mech.energy.power`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Energy  
**Difficulty:** 1/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.energy.work

## Core Concepts
- power
- average vs instantaneous power
- efficiency

## Key Equations
$$
P_{avg} = \frac{W}{\Delta t}
$$
$$
P = \vec F\cdot\vec v
$$
$$
\eta = \frac{P_{out}}{P_{in}}
$$

## Methods
- Use P = dW/dt for time-varying work
- Use P = Fv when force and velocity are known
- Track energy losses for efficiency

## Typical Problem Types
- Power needed to lift a load
- Car engine power at a given speed
- Efficiency of a machine

## Common Pitfalls
- Mixing average and instantaneous power
- Using P = Fv with non-parallel F and v
