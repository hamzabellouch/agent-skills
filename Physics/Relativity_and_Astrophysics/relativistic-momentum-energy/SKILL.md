# Relativistic Momentum and Energy

**ID:** `rel.special.momentum_energy`  
**Domain:** Relativity → Special  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- rel.special.lorentz

## Core Concepts
- relativistic momentum
- rest energy
- total energy
- mass-energy equivalence
- E-p relation

## Key Equations
$$
\vec p = \gamma m\vec v
$$
$$
E = \gamma mc^2
$$
$$
E^2 = (pc)^2 + (mc^2)^2
$$

## Methods
- Use the relativistic momentum and energy expressions
- Apply conservation laws in relativistic collisions
- Use the invariant mass

## Typical Problem Types
- Relativistic collision kinematics
- Pair production threshold
- Decay kinematics

## Common Pitfalls
- Using KE = ½mv² at high speeds
- Confusing rest mass and relativistic mass
