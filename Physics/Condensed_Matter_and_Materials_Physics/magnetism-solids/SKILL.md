# Magnetism in Solids

**ID:** `cond.magnetism`  
**Domain:** Condensed Matter → Magnetic Order  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- stat.mech.ising
- qm.formalism.spin

## Core Concepts
- diamagnetism
- paramagnetism
- ferromagnetism
- Curie law
- exchange interaction
- domains

## Key Equations
$$
\chi = \frac{C}{T}\ \text{(Curie)}
$$
$$
\chi = \frac{C}{T-T_c}\ \text{(Curie-Weiss)}
$$

## Methods
- Use the Curie and Curie-Weiss laws
- Distinguish dia/para/ferro/antiferro
- Apply mean-field theory for ferromagnetism

## Typical Problem Types
- Paramagnetic susceptibility
- Ferromagnetic transition
- Magnetic domains and hysteresis

## Common Pitfalls
- Using classical dipoles only
- Confusing susceptibility definitions
