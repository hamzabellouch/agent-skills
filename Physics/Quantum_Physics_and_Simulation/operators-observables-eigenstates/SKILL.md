# Operators, Observables and Eigenstates

**ID:** `qm.formalism.operators`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Formalism  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.schrodinger.wavefunction
- math.linear_algebra

## Core Concepts
- Hermitian operators
- commutators
- eigenvalues
- expectation values
- compatible observables

## Key Equations
$$
[\hat x,\hat p] = i\hbar
$$
$$
\langle A\rangle = \langle\psi|\hat A|\psi\rangle
$$
$$
[\hat A,\hat B]=0 \Leftrightarrow \text{simultaneous eigenbasis}
$$

## Methods
- Check hermiticity
- Compute commutators
- Diagonalize the relevant operator

## Typical Problem Types
- Compatible observables
- Expectation values in given states
- Heisenberg picture vs Schrödinger picture

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting operator ordering
- Assuming every pair commutes
