## Key Equations
$$
\sigma_f^2 = \sum_i \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}\right)^2\sigma_{x_i}^2
$$

## Methods
- Choose a distribution model
- Fit parameters via maximum likelihood
- Report uncertainties

- Fitting a line to data

## Common Pitfalls
