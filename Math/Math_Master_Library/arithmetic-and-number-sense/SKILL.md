# Arithmetic & Number Sense

## Core skills
- Order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS): parentheses, exponents, multiplication/division left to right, addition/subtraction left to right.
- Fractions: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd; multiply straight across; divide by multiplying by the reciprocal; simplify with gcd.
- Percent: x% of y = xy/100; percent change = (new - old)/old x 100; successive changes multiply (+20% then -20% = -4%).
- Ratios/proportions: a/b = c/d -> ad = bc; unit rates; scale factors; inverse proportion xy = k.
- Exponents: a^m a^n = a^(m+n); (a^m)^n = a^(mn); a^0 = 1; a^(-n) = 1/a^n; a^(1/n) = nth root.
- Scientific notation: a x 10^n with 1 <= a < 10; multiply mantissas and add exponents.
- Divisibility: 2 (last digit even), 3 (digit sum), 4 (last two digits), 5, 9 (digit sum), 11 (alternating sum).
- Rounding and significant figures; estimation by rounding to 1-2 digits; bound the error.

## Mental-math tricks
- x5: halve then x10. x11: add neighboring digits. Square ending in 5: n5^2 = n(n+1) followed by 25.
- (a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2 for near-equal products (47 x 53 = 50^2 - 3^2 = 2491).
- Percent swap: x% of y = y% of x.

## Word-problem method
1. Underline the unknown. 2. Assign a variable with units. 3. Translate sentences to equations. 4. Solve. 5. Check in the original wording and sanity-check the magnitude.

## Pitfalls
Dividing by a fraction, negative signs with exponents (-3^2 = -9 vs (-3)^2 = 9), percent of vs percent more than, unit conversions.
