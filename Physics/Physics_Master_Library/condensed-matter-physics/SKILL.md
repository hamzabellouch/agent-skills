# Condensed Matter Physics

## Structure
- Bravais lattices (14 in 3D); Miller indices; reciprocal lattice G·R = 2πn; Brillouin zones
- Diffraction: Bragg 2d sinθ = nλ; Laue k' - k = G; structure factor; X-ray, neutron, electron probes

## Vibrations
- Monatomic chain ω = 2 sqrt(K/m)|sin(ka/2)|; diatomic chain acoustic and optical branches
- Phonons as bosons; Debye model θ_D, C ∝ T³; thermal conductivity; anharmonicity

## Electrons
- Drude σ = ne²τ/m; Sommerfeld free-electron gas; Fermi energy, DOS g(E) ∝ √E in 3D
- Bloch theorem ψ_k = e^{ik·r}u_k(r); nearly-free electrons open gaps at zone boundaries; tight binding E(k) = ε0 - 2t cos(ka)
- Effective mass m* = ħ²/(d²E/dk²); holes; semiclassical dynamics ħk̇ = -eE - ev×B; cyclotron resonance, de Haas-van Alphen
- Metals, insulators, semiconductors from band filling; Hall effect R_H = 1/(nq)

## Many-body & magnetism
- Second quantization; Hubbard model; Mott insulators; Fermi-liquid theory; Luttinger liquids in 1D
- Magnetism: Heisenberg model J S_i·S_j, spin waves (magnons), Stoner criterion, RKKY, Kondo effect, frustrated magnets, spin liquids

## Superconductivity & superfluidity
- Meissner effect; London penetration depth λ_L; coherence length ξ; type I/II; flux quantum Φ0 = h/2e
- BCS: Cooper pairs, gap Δ ≈ 1.76 k_B T_c; Ginzburg-Landau theory; Josephson effect I = I_c sin φ, V = (ħ/2e)φ̇; SQUIDs
- High-Tc cuprates, iron-based, nickelates, hydrides (claims require careful reproducibility checks)
- He-4 superfluid (Landau criterion), He-3 p-wave pairing

## Topology and low-dimensional physics
- Integer quantum Hall σ_xy = νe²/h; fractional QHE (Laughlin states, anyons)
- Berry curvature, Chern number, topological insulators (surface Dirac cones), Weyl semimetals, graphene (Dirac points, v_F ≈ 10⁶ m/s), moiré/twisted bilayers, Majorana modes
- Quantum phase transitions; Mermin-Wagner theorem; Kosterlitz-Thouless transition
