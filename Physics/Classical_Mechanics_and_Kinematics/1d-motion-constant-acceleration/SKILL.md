## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
v = v_0 + at
$$
$$
x = x_0 + v_0 t + \tfrac12 a t^2
$$
$$
v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x-x_0)
$$
$$
x = x_0 + \tfrac12(v_0+v)t
$$

## Step-by-Step Analytical & Problem-Solving Methods
1. **Identify knowns/unknowns and choose the suvat equation missing the unwanted variable**
2. **Split motion into phases when acceleration changes**
3. **Use g = 9.81 m/s^2 downward for free fall**
