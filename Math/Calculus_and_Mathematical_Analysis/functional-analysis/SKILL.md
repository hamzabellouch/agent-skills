# Functional Analysis

## Domain
Infinite-dimensional vector spaces and operators.

## Core Concepts
- Normed, Banach, Hilbert spaces
- Bounded and compact operators
- Dual spaces, weak topologies
- Spectral theory
- Distributions

## Key Methods
- Hahn–Banach theorem
- Open mapping, closed graph theorems
- Riesz representation
- Spectral theorem for self-adjoint operators

## Canonical Examples
- L²[a,b] is a Hilbert space.
- Fourier series as orthogonal expansion.
- Compact operators have discrete spectra.

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming finite-dimensional intuition carries
- Confusing weak and strong convergence
- Ignoring completeness

## Related Skills
Real Analysis, PDEs, Quantum Mechanics
