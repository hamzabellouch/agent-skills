## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
Z_R = R
$$
$$
Z_L = i\omega L
$$
$$
Z_C = \frac{1}{i\omega C}
$$
$$
Z_{RLC} = \sqrt{R^2 + (\omega L - 1/\omega C)^2}
$$
