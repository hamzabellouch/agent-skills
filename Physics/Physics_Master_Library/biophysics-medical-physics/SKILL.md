# Biophysics & Medical Physics

## Cellular & molecular
- Diffusion: ⟨x²⟩ = 2Dt (1D); Stokes-Einstein D = k_BT/6πηr; low Reynolds number — viscosity dominates, reciprocal motion doesn't propel (Purcell)
- Boltzmann factors govern binding, two-state models, Hill equation θ = cⁿ/(Kⁿ + cⁿ); Michaelis-Menten v = Vmax[S]/(Km+[S])
- Polymers: ideal chain R ~ N^{1/2}b, worm-like chain persistence length (DNA ≈ 50 nm), entropic elasticity
- Membranes: Nernst E = (kT/zq) ln(c_out/c_in); Goldman-Hodgkin-Katz; Hodgkin-Huxley model of action potentials; cable equation; capacitance ~1 μF/cm²
- Molecular motors: kinesin steps 8 nm with ~6 pN stall force, ATP ≈ 20 k_BT; ratchet models; F1-ATPase rotary
- Protein folding: energy landscape, funnel; Levinthal paradox; AlphaFold-type structure prediction (data-driven)
- Single-molecule techniques: optical tweezers, AFM, FRET, cryo-EM, super-resolution (STORM, STED, PALM)

## Organism scale
- Biomechanics: bone stress, muscle force-length, gait, blood flow (Poiseuille, Windkessel), heart as pump, scaling laws
- Sensory physics: vision photon detection threshold, hearing cochlea tonotopy, thermoregulation

## Medical imaging & therapy
- X-ray/CT: attenuation I = I0 e^{-μx}, Hounsfield units, filtered back-projection
- MRI: Larmor f = γB (42.58 MHz/T for H), T1/T2 relaxation, gradient encoding, k-space, fMRI BOLD
- Nuclear medicine: PET (positron annihilation 511 keV pairs), SPECT, radiotracers
- Ultrasound imaging, optical coherence tomography
- Radiotherapy: Bragg peak for protons, dose-volume histograms, linear-quadratic cell survival model, units Gy/Sv
- Safety and ALARA principle
