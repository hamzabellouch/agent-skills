## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
L = n\hbar
$$
$$
r_n = \frac{4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar^2}{m_e e^2} n^2 = n^2 a_0
$$
$$
E_n = -\frac{m_e e^4}{32\pi^2\epsilon_0^2\hbar^2} \frac{1}{n^2} = -\frac{13.6\text{ eV}}{n^2}
$$
$$
\frac{1}{\lambda} = R_\infty \left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)
$$
