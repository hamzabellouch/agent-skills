# Identical Particles and the Pauli Principle

**ID:** `qm.many_body.pauli`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Many-Body  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.formalism.spin

## Core Concepts
- bosons
- fermions
- Slater determinant
- exchange symmetry
- Pauli exclusion

## Key Equations
$$
\psi(1,2) = -\psi(2,1)\ \text{(fermions)}
$$
$$
\psi(1,2) = +\psi(2,1)\ \text{(bosons)}
$$

## Methods
- Symmetrize/antisymmetrize the total wavefunction
- Build Slater determinants
- Apply Pauli exclusion

## Typical Problem Types
- Helium atom energy levels
- Periodic table structure
- Exchange energy

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting spin part in the symmetry
- Treating electrons as distinguishable
