# Banked Curves and Turning Vehicles

**ID:** `mech.dynamics.banked_curves`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Dynamics  
**Difficulty:** 3/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.dynamics.centripetal
- mech.dynamics.friction

## Core Concepts
- banking angle
- design speed
- frictionless banking
- combined friction and banking

## Key Equations
$$
\tan\theta = \frac{v^2}{rg}\ \text{(frictionless)}
$$
$$
N = \frac{mg}{\cos\theta - \mu_s\sin\theta}\ \text{(with friction)}
$$

## Methods
- Draw the FBD with normal perpendicular to the road
- Resolve into horizontal (radial) and vertical components
- Solve for the target quantity

## Typical Problem Types
- Find the design speed of a banked curve
- Range of speeds with friction

## Common Pitfalls
- Setting N = mgcos	heta on a banked curve
- Forgetting that the normal has a horizontal component
