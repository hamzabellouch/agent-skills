# Orbital Motion and Kepler's Laws

**ID:** `mech.gravitation.orbits_kepler`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Gravitation  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.gravitation.field_potential
- mech.rotation.ang_momentum_conservation

## Core Concepts
- Kepler's laws
- circular orbit
- elliptical orbit
- vis-viva equation
- orbital energy

## Key Equations
$$
T^2 \propto a^3
$$
$$
\frac{dA}{dt} = \text{const}
$$
$$
E = -\frac{GMm}{2a}
$$
$$
v^2 = GM\left(\frac{2}{r}-\frac{1}{a}\right)
$$

## Methods
- Use vis-viva for speed at any radius
- Use energy and angular momentum conservation
- Apply Kepler's third law for periods

## Typical Problem Types
- Circular orbit speed
- Elliptical orbit energy
- Satellite transfer orbits

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming circular when the orbit is elliptical
- Forgetting that a is the semi-major axis
