## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
v_p = \frac{\omega}{k}
$$
$$
v_g = \frac{d\omega}{dk}
$$
$$
v_g v_p = c^2\ \text{(non-relativistic matter waves)}
$$

## Step-by-Step Analytical & Problem-Solving Methods
1. **Determine \omega(k) from the medium**
2. **Compute group velocity from the dispersion relation**
