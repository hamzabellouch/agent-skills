# Proof & Competition Methods

## Polya's four steps
1. Understand (restate, small cases, draw). 2. Plan (analogy, auxiliary problem, work backward, special cases, symmetry, generalize). 3. Execute and verify each step. 4. Look back (alternate proof, extensions).

## Technique catalog
- Induction (weak, strong, double, Cauchy forward-backward), minimal counterexample / infinite descent.
- Pigeonhole, double counting, bijections, invariants/monovariants, coloring, extremal principle, constructive existence, probabilistic method.
- Inequalities: AM-GM (a+b)/2 >= sqrt(ab); Cauchy-Schwarz (sum a_i b_i)^2 <= (sum a_i^2)(sum b_i^2); Titu/Engel; rearrangement; Jensen; Schur; Muirhead; SOS; substitution a = x/y; tangent-line trick; equality cases always.
- Number theory: mod small numbers (squares mod 4, 8, cubes mod 9), LTE, order arguments, Vieta jumping, factoring (Simon's favorite factoring trick).
- Algebra: symmetric polynomials, Vieta, telescoping, functional equations (substitute x=0,1,-1; injectivity; surjectivity; Cauchy's equation f(x+y) = f(x) + f(y)).
- Geometry: angle chasing, power of a point, spiral similarity, inversion, coordinates/complex/barycentric bash, trigonometric Ceva.
- Combinatorics: generating functions, recursion, states and graphs, games (strategy stealing, symmetry, P/N positions).

## Proof writing standards
- State what is assumed and what is to be shown; define notation; every step justified by a definition, theorem, or earlier line.
- Avoid circular reasoning, check division by zero, domain issues, and the quantifier order.
- Cover all cases; conclude explicitly; if you cannot prove something fully, say which gap remains.

## Verification habit
Test conjectures on small cases (n = 1..6), look for counterexamples, then prove. If a formula is guessed, prove it by induction.
