# Partial Differential Equations (PDEs)

## Domain
Equations involving partial derivatives.

## Core Concepts
- Classification: elliptic, parabolic, hyperbolic
- Boundary and initial conditions
- Well-posedness
- Weak solutions, distributions

## Key Methods
- Separation of variables
- Fourier and Laplace transforms
- Method of characteristics
- Green's functions
- Finite difference / finite element (numerical)

## Canonical Examples
- Heat equation: u_t = α u_xx.
- Wave equation: u_tt = c² u_xx.
- Laplace equation: ∇²u = 0.

## Common Pitfalls
- Wrong boundary conditions leading to non-uniqueness
- Mixing up wave vs. heat behavior
- Ignoring regularity requirements

ODEs, Functional Analysis, Numerical Methods, Physics
