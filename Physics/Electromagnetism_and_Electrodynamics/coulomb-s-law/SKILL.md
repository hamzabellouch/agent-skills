## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
\vec F = k\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}\hat r

$$
k = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \approx 8.99\times10^9\ \text{N m}^2/\text{C}^2
$$
