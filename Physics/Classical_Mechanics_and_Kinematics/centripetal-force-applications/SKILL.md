## Governing Equations & Mathematical Formulations
$$
\sum F_{radial} = \frac{mv^2}{r}
$$
$$
v_{min} = \sqrt{gR}\ \text{(vertical circle top)}
$$

## Step-by-Step Analytical & Problem-Solving Methods
1. **Identify the physical force(s) acting radially**
2. **Set their resultant equal to mv^2/r**
3. **Check the minimum-speed condition**
