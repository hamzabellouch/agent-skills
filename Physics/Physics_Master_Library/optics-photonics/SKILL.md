# Optics & Photonics

## Geometric optics
- Snell: n1 sinθ1 = n2 sinθ2; thin lens 1/f = 1/s + 1/s'; m = -s'/s; lensmaker 1/f = (n-1)(1/R1 - 1/R2)
- Mirror f = R/2; ray transfer (ABCD) matrices for systems and cavities
- Aberrations: spherical, chromatic, coma, astigmatism; Abbe number
- Instruments: magnifier M = 25cm/f; telescope M = -f_o/f_e; microscope; resolving limit

## Wave optics
- Double slit: bright fringes d sinθ = mλ; fringe spacing Δy = λL/d
- Single slit minima a sinθ = mλ; circular aperture (Rayleigh) θ = 1.22λ/D
- Grating: d sinθ = mλ; resolving power R = mN
- Thin film: 2nt = (m + ½)λ for constructive when one phase flip
- Michelson/Fabry-Perot: finesse F = π√R/(1-R); free spectral range c/2L
- Fraunhofer diffraction = Fourier transform of aperture; Fresnel number N_F = a²/λL
- Coherence: length l_c = c/Δν; spatial coherence; van Cittert-Zernike

## Polarization
- Jones calculus; Malus: I = I0 cos²θ; waveplates λ/2, λ/4; Stokes vectors and Poincaré sphere; birefringence Δn

## Lasers & photonics
- Einstein coefficients; gain condition (population inversion); threshold; cavity modes f = mc/2L
- Gaussian beam: w(z) = w0 sqrt(1+(z/z_R)²); z_R = πw0²/λ; divergence θ = λ/πw0
- Optical fiber: NA = sqrt(n_core²-n_clad²); single-mode V = (2πa/λ)NA < 2.405; dispersion and attenuation
- Nonlinear: second harmonic generation, phase matching, Kerr effect, self-focusing
- Photodetection shot noise; quantum optics: photon statistics (Poissonian, thermal, sub-Poissonian), squeezed states, Hong-Ou-Mandel effect

## Tip
State the regime first (ray vs wave; paraxial; coherent vs incoherent) then pick the toolbox.
