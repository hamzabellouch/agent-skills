# Geometry & Trigonometry

## Plane geometry
- Triangle: angle sum 180; area = 1/2 bh = 1/2 ab sinC = sqrt(s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)) (Heron); inradius r = Area/s; circumradius R = abc/(4 Area).
- Pythagoras a^2 + b^2 = c^2; special triangles 30-60-90 (1, sqrt3, 2), 45-45-90.
- Similarity: AA, SAS, SSS; area ratio = (scale)^2. Congruence: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL.
- Centers: centroid (2:1 on medians), incenter, circumcenter, orthocenter; Euler line.
- Circle: C = 2 pi r, A = pi r^2; inscribed angle = 1/2 central angle; tangent perpendicular to radius; power of a point; chords: ab = cd.
- Polygons: interior angle sum (n-2)180; regular n-gon area = 1/2 (perimeter)(apothem).
- Theorems: Angle bisector, Menelaus, Ceva, Ptolemy (cyclic quadrilateral ac + bd = pq), Stewart.

## Solid geometry
Sphere V = 4/3 pi r^3, S = 4 pi r^2; cone V = 1/3 pi r^2 h; cylinder V = pi r^2 h; pyramid V = 1/3 Bh; Euler V - E + F = 2.

## Coordinate geometry
Distance, midpoint, slope, line equations (point-slope), circle (x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2, conics: ellipse x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1, parabola, hyperbola; shoelace formula for polygon area.

## Trigonometry
- Definitions SOH-CAH-TOA; unit circle; radians: 180 deg = pi rad.
- Identities: sin^2 + cos^2 = 1; sin(a+b) = sin a cos b + cos a sin b; cos(a+b) = cos a cos b - sin a sin b; double angle sin 2a = 2 sin a cos a, cos 2a = cos^2 a - sin^2 a; sum-to-product; tan = sin/cos.
- Law of sines a/sinA = 2R; law of cosines c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab cosC. Ambiguous case SSA may give 0, 1, or 2 triangles.
- Graphs: amplitude, period 2 pi/b, phase shift; inverse trig ranges (arcsin [-pi/2, pi/2], arccos [0, pi], arctan (-pi/2, pi/2)).
- Euler: e^(i theta) = cos theta + i sin theta; De Moivre.

## Method
Draw an accurate diagram, label knowns, look for right triangles / similar triangles / cyclic quadrilaterals, try coordinates or complex numbers when synthetic proof stalls.
