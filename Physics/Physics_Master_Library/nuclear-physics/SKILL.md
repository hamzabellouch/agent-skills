# Nuclear Physics

## Structure
- Radius R ≈ 1.2 A^{1/3} fm; density ≈ 2.3e17 kg/m³
- Semi-empirical mass formula: B = a_V A - a_S A^{2/3} - a_C Z(Z-1)/A^{1/3} - a_A (A-2Z)²/A ± δ; peaks near Fe-56 (≈ 8.8 MeV/nucleon)
- Shell model magic numbers 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126; spin-orbit coupling; collective models (liquid drop, rotational, vibrational); halo nuclei; island of stability

## Decay
- N(t) = N0 e^{-λt}, t½ = ln2/λ, mean life τ = 1/λ; activity A = λN (1 Bq = 1 decay/s; 1 Ci = 3.7e10 Bq)
- Chains: Bateman equations; secular equilibrium when λ_parent ≪ λ_daughter
- α decay (Gamow tunneling, Geiger-Nuttall), β⁻/β⁺/EC (neutrino, Q-value, Fermi theory), γ decay and internal conversion
- Q = (m_initial - m_final)c²; use atomic masses carefully for β decay

## Reactions
- Cross section σ (barn = 1e-28 m²); rate R = nσΦ; Q-value and threshold; compound nucleus; resonances
- Fission: U-235 thermal ≈ 200 MeV per fission, ν ≈ 2.4 neutrons; chain reaction k_eff = 1 critical; delayed neutrons; moderators, control rods; breeding (U-238 → Pu-239)
- Fusion: p-p chain, CNO cycle, D-T reaction (17.6 MeV); Coulomb barrier, Gamow peak; Lawson criterion nTτ ≳ 3e21 keV·s/m³; tokamaks, stellarators, inertial confinement
- Nucleosynthesis: Big Bang, stellar, s-process, r-process (neutron star mergers)

## Radiation & dosimetry
- Interaction: α (short range), β (range), γ (exponential attenuation I = I0 e^{-μx}, HVL = ln2/μ), neutrons
- Photoelectric, Compton (Δλ = (h/mc)(1 - cosθ)), pair production (>1.022 MeV)
- Dose: gray (J/kg), sievert (equivalent dose); detectors: Geiger, scintillator, semiconductor, cloud/bubble chambers
- Applications: PET, radiotherapy, radiocarbon dating (t½ = 5730 y), medical isotopes
