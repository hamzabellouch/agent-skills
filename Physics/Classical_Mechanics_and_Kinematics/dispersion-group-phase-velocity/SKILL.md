# Dispersion, Group and Phase Velocity

**ID:** `mech.waves.dispersion`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Oscillations & Waves  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.waves.wave_equation

## Core Concepts
- dispersion relation
- group velocity
- phase velocity
- wave packet
- envelope

## Key Equations
$$
v_p = \frac{\omega}{k}
$$
$$
v_g = \frac{d\omega}{dk}
$$
$$
v_g v_p = c^2\ \text{(non-relativistic matter waves)}
$$

## Methods
- Determine \omega(k) from the medium
- Compute group velocity from the dispersion relation
- Use v_g for energy/signal transport

## Typical Problem Types
- Water waves
- EM waves in a medium
- Quantum wave packets

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing v_p and v_g
- Assuming non-dispersive behavior
