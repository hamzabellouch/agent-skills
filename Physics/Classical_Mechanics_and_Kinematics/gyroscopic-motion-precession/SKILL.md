# Gyroscopic Motion and Precession

**ID:** `mech.rotation.gyroscope`  
**Domain:** Mechanics → Rotation  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- mech.rotation.ang_momentum

## Core Concepts
- gyroscope
- precession
- nutation
- torque-induced precession

## Key Equations
$$
\vec\Omega_p = \frac{\tau}{L}
$$
$$
\vec\tau = \vec\Omega_p\times\vec L
$$

## Methods
- Identify L along the spin axis
- Compute the torque from gravity
- Use \Omega_p = 	au / L

## Typical Problem Types
- Spinning top
- Bicycle wheel gyroscope
- Precession of Earth's axis

## Common Pitfalls
- Thinking precession is instantaneous
- Forgetting that L and 	au must be perpendicular for simple precession
