# Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

**ID:** `qm.found.uncertainty`  
**Domain:** Quantum Mechanics → Foundations  
**Difficulty:** 4/5

## Prerequisites
- qm.schrodinger.wavefunction

## Core Concepts
- uncertainty principle
- conjugate variables
- energy-time uncertainty
- wave packets

## Key Equations
$$
\Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}
$$
$$
\Delta E\,\Delta t \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}
$$

## Methods
- Identify the conjugate pair
- Use it to estimate ground-state energies
- Interpret energy widths of short-lived states

## Typical Problem Types
- Ground state of hydrogen via uncertainty
- Natural linewidth
- Virtual particle lifetimes

## Common Pitfalls
- Treating it as a measurement limitation only
- Using \hbar instead of \hbar/2
